# Crazy Wizard Syndrome

**Live site:** https://sauerninja.github.io/Crazy-Wizard/

A small, single-page educational resource about the real psychological risks of doing intensive esoteric, occult, or "hidden knowledge" research entirely alone — and why every historical tradition built around this material (mystery schools, Hermetic orders, Kabbalistic study circles, alchemical guilds, monastic contemplative schools) required a mentor or a peer group instead.

## What this is

Solitary, symbolic, paradigm-dismantling research is a known destabilizer in the clinical literature — not because the subject matter is "dangerous" in some mystical sense, but because it removes a specific safety mechanism: an external party who can say *"you're stretching that connection too far."* This site walks through:

- **Apophenia** — why symbolic material is fertile ground for over-reading patterns, grounded in Klaus Conrad's 1958 work on prodromal psychosis (*Trema* / *Apophänie*)
- **Overvalued idea vs. delusion** — Karl Jaspers' clinical distinction (1913), which turns on whether doubt survives, not on how strange a belief sounds
- **What the actual research shows** — the DSM-5's V62.89 "Religious or Spiritual Problem" category and Brown University's decade-long *Varieties of Contemplative Experience* study (Britton & Lindahl), including documented delusion-like ideation in isolated practitioners
- **Self-check signs** it's time to bring someone else into the process — reflection prompts, not a diagnostic tool
- **Concrete countermeasures** — reconstructing what a mentor/community actually provided, without needing to join an order

This is a general-audience educational resource. It does not diagnose anyone, and it isn't a substitute for talking to a real person or a licensed professional.

## Sourcing

Every clinical claim on the site is tied to a real, verifiable source (Conrad, Jaspers, DSM-5, the Brown CLANlab VCE study) — see the References section at the bottom of the page. Claims that couldn't be verified against a real source were left out rather than included on the strength of an AI summary.
